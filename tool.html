Upload a blood cell image

Choose a stained blood smear image. It stays on your device and is compared with four learned white blood cell patterns.

  • Use one white blood cell near the centre, not a whole field of cells or a screenshot.
  • Works best on stained (Wright or Giemsa-type) smears at high magnification.
